Construction Costs in Sicily 2026: A Price Guide per Square Metre

By Arch. Fabio Costanzo, Studio 4e · Updated: March 2026

Quoting a single price per square metre for construction in Sicily without specifying location is meaningless — the island operates on three distinct price levels that vary by 40–60%. Here is the 2026 reference table by zone, plus the costs most budgets miss.

Sicily's three construction markets

  • Palermo and Catania: highest labour costs, best availability of specialist trades, most competitive market for high-end finishes
  • Tourist coast and UNESCO towns (Taormina, Cefalù, Ortigia, Ragusa Ibla, Noto, Scicli): premium labour driven by short-term rental demand; specialist trades scarce and expensive
  • Inland provinces (Enna, Caltanissetta, interior Agrigento): lowest labour costs in Sicily, but reduced availability of specialist contractors

These figures cover materials and labour for construction works only. They exclude: architect/engineer fees (8–14%), permit costs, furniture, appliances, landscaping, and VAT (10% on renovation of primary residence, 22% on second homes and new builds).

The costs most Sicilian renovation budgets miss

  • Demolition and waste disposal: removing existing tiles, plasterwork and sanitary ware produces significant rubble volumes. Budget €15–35/m² for demolition and licensed disposal
  • Asbestos removal: buildings pre-1992 may contain asbestos in roof sheets, pipe insulation or floor tiles. Budget €30–80/m² of affected surface for certified removal and disposal
  • Provisional works and site setup: scaffolding (€15–30/m² of facade for the rental period), temporary water/electricity connections, site insurance (€500–1,500/year), mandatory safety coordinator on multi-contractor sites
  • Structural surprises: in buildings pre-1980, structural condition is never fully known until opened. Reserve 10–15% contingency for structural findings
  • Seismic upgrade requirement: if renovation exceeds 25% of the building's reconstruction value or involves significant structural works, Italian regulations may trigger mandatory seismic upgrading. In Sicily (zone 2), this can add €200–500/m² to the total

How to get a reliable estimate

The only reliable cost estimate for a specific Sicilian property comes from a bill of quantities (computo metrico) prepared by an architect after an on-site survey. Rough per-square-metre estimates are useful for initial feasibility; they are not reliable for budgeting or contractor selection. Studio 4e's process: survey → project → computo metrico → invite 3 contractors to quote on identical scope → compare voce per voce.

Renovation incentives still available in 2026

  • Bonus Ristrutturazioni (50%): 50% IRPEF deduction on eligible renovation works up to €96,000 per unit, deducted over 10 years. Applies to main residence and rental properties. Requires Italian income tax to absorb.
  • Ecobonus (50–65%): for energy efficiency improvements (insulation, new heating, windows)
  • Sismabonus (50–85%): for seismic improvement in zones 1, 2 and 3. Sicily fully qualifies. Higher rates if the building moves up two or more seismic risk classes
