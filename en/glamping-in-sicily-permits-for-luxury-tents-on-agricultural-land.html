Glamping in Sicily: Permits for Luxury Tents on Agricultural Land

By Arch. Fabio Costanzo, Studio 4e · Updated: March 2026

Urgent question? Call: +39 329 973 6697

Glamping — permanently-erected luxury tents, safari tents, or geodesic domes on agricultural land — is growing rapidly as a hospitality format in Sicily. The regulatory framework is genuinely complex: it sits at the intersection of agriturismo law (Legge Regionale n.6/2001 and its 2022 update), camping regulations, tourist accommodation classification, and agricultural land use rules. Getting this right upfront saves years of regulatory problems.

The three legal frameworks glamping can fall into

The legal classification of a glamping operation in Sicily determines which permits you need, which taxes you pay, and what minimum standards apply. The three possible frameworks:

1. Agriturismo: agricultural accommodation where the primary activity is farming. Under Legge Regionale Siciliana n.6/2001 (agriturismo law), the accommodation component must be subordinate to agricultural production — the farmer must derive the majority of their income from farming, not from hosting. Maximum number of accommodation units varies (currently up to 30 beds in a standard agriturismo, up to 50 with special authorisation). Advantage: lower planning barriers for temporary structures on agricultural land, preferred tax treatment (imprenditore agricolo regime for IRPEF). Disadvantage: must demonstrate active agricultural production; a property with no farming activity cannot use the agriturismo framework.

2. Camping/glamping site (area attrezzata): regulated as a commercial tourist accommodation facility under Legge Regionale Siciliana n.27/1985 (strutture ricettive all'aria aperta). Minimum standards include: defined pitch dimensions, toilet blocks per pitch ratio, access roads, fire prevention infrastructure. A formal classification (1–4 stars for camping sites) is required. Planning permission for the new infrastructure is required as a tourist facility, not as agricultural use. Higher regulatory complexity but more freedom from agricultural income requirements.

3. Country house / villa rental with accessory tent accommodation (casa vacanze): if the glamping tents are accessory to an existing residential property (rather than the primary accommodation product), some municipalities allow them under the general holiday rental (casa vacanze) framework without requiring camping site classification. Maximum typically 5–6 tents. This is the simplest framework for a property owner who wants to add modest glamping to an existing vacation rental operation.

What "temporary structure" means for planning purposes

The key regulatory question for any glamping tent is whether it is classified as a permanent or temporary structure. Italian planning law draws the line based on durability, not removability: a tent that is erected and dismantled seasonally (April–October) and stored in a warehouse the rest of the year is typically treated as temporary. A tent that remains in place year-round, even if technically movable, may be treated as a permanent structure and trigger full building permit requirements.

The Regione Siciliana's implementing regulations for glamping structures (Circolare assessoriale n.2/2022) allow seasonal tent structures (strutture temporanee stagionali) for tourist accommodation without building permits, subject to:

  • Maximum footprint of 60 sqm per tent unit
  • Maximum height of 5m
  • Timber or metal frame (no reinforced concrete foundations — timber posts or ground anchors only)
  • Not connected to permanent drainage (compostable toilet or connection to a septic system of the main building)
  • Seasonality declaration (dichiarazione di stagionalità) filed with the SUE

Permanent glamping structures (hard-sided safari cabins, geodesic domes on concrete pads, container conversions) are treated as permanent construction regardless of their glamping marketing — they require standard building permits and compliance with all planning rules for the zone in question.

The agriturismo income test: the critical constraint for agricultural land glamping

If you want to use the agriturismo framework (usually the most permissive for agricultural land), you must pass the income test: agricultural income must be greater than agriturismo income. The Regione Siciliana enforces this through annual declarations to the Regione Assessorato Regionale dell'Agricoltura.

For a glamping business generating €80,000/year in tent rental income, the associated agricultural activity must generate more than €80,000 in agricultural income — this typically requires significant olive, citrus or wine production. For a small landowner with 2–3 hectares of agricultural land, this income test may be difficult to pass if the glamping operation becomes the primary revenue driver.

A common structure used by Studio 4e clients: maintain the agricultural activity (olive grove, citrus orchard) as the primary income, and limit glamping capacity to 4–6 tents generating income subordinate to the agricultural production. This keeps the agriturismo classification active while building the glamping business incrementally.

Infrastructure requirements: the minimum you need to be legal

For a 4-tent glamping operation using the agriturismo framework, the minimum infrastructure that regulators and guests expect:

  • Water supply: potable water to each tent, either from the mains network or a certified private well (pozzo artesiano) with water quality testing per D.Lgs 31/2001. A buried water line from the main building to each tent platform: €2,000–4,000 in civil works.
  • Drainage: each tent requires a drainage point for shower and basin water. For remote locations without mains drainage, a centralised imhoff septic tank (for 4 tents of 2 persons each = 8 persons) requires a tank capacity of approximately 2,000 litres plus a percolation field. Installation: €3,000–6,000. An environmental engineer (tecnico ambientale) must size and certify the system before installation.
  • Electricity: each tent requires at least 3 kW of electrical supply for lighting, phone charging and a small climate control unit. Underground supply cable from the main building: €1,500–3,000 depending on distance.
  • Solid waste: waste from guest accommodation is classified as residential waste (rifiuti urbani assimilati agli urbani) and must be separated and collected by the municipal waste service. Register the property with the comune for commercial waste tariff (TARI commerciale).

Tourist accommodation classification and the CIR code

Like all Sicilian tourist accommodation, glamping tents used for short-term rental must be registered with the Regione Siciliana and obtain a CIR (Codice Identificativo Regionale) number. For agriturismo-classified operations, registration is through the Regione's agriturismo office. For camping/glamping sites, through the tourist accommodation register.

The CIR number must be displayed in all online listings (Airbnb, Booking.com, direct website) under Sicilian regulations updated in 2022. Listing without a CIR is subject to fines of €500–5,000 per listing.

Tax treatment of glamping income

Agriturismo income is taxed under the agricultural income regime (reddito agrario), which uses a flat-rate forfettario calculation based on the registered agricultural land value (rendita catastale agraria) multiplied by a coefficient, rather than actual revenue. This is extremely favourable compared to ordinary income tax — an agriturismo generating €100,000 in revenue may pay effective income tax on €8,000–15,000 of deemed income. For an IRPEF taxpayer in the 35% bracket, this represents a substantial tax saving.

For foreign owners: the agriturismo forfettario regime requires Italian residency (iscrizione AIRE or Italian residenza anagrafica). Non-resident foreign owners cannot directly access the agriturismo regime. A common solution: the Italian spouse or partner registers as the agricultural entrepreneur (imprenditore agricolo), while the foreign owner holds the property. Requires careful legal structuring with an Italian commercialista and avvocato.
